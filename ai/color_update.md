MAJOR THEME UPDATE:
Based on the current date, use the following colors:
Color,Days / Season
Green,Ordinary Time: Used for the majority of the year (between Christmas/Lent and Easter/Advent).
White,"Christmas & Easter Seasons: Plus Feasts of Jesus, Mary, and Saints who were not martyrs. Also used for Weddings and Baptisms."
Red,"Passion & Holy Spirit: Palm Sunday, Good Friday, Pentecost, and Feast Days of Martyrs and Apostles."
Purple,Advent & Lent: Also used for the Sacrament of Reconciliation (Confession) and often for Funerals.
Rose,Special Sundays: Only used on Gaudete Sunday (3rd Sunday of Advent) and Laetare Sunday (4th Sunday of Lent).
Black,Mourning: Optional for All Souls' Day (Nov 2) and Funeral Masses.

Use modern color pallets for the glassmorphism effect.

Background should be diffused theme color with a heavily blurred glassmorphism effect.

All menus should be a more transparent glassmorphism effect. Add a dash of color where appropriate, but do not overdo it. No hard colors, all glassmorphism colors.
