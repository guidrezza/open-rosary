The Joyful Mysteries
The Annunciation: Humility

The Visitation: Love of Neighbor (Charity)

The Nativity: Poverty of Spirit (Detachment from the world)

The Presentation: Obedience

The Finding in the Temple: Piety (Joy in finding Jesus)

The Luminous Mysteries
The Baptism in the Jordan: Openness to the Holy Spirit

The Wedding at Cana: To Jesus through Mary

The Proclamation of the Kingdom: Repentance and Trust in God

The Transfiguration: Desire for Holiness

The Institution of the Eucharist: Adoration (Active Participation in the Mass)

The Sorrowful Mysteries
The Agony in the Garden: Sorrow for Sin (Conformity to God's Will)

The Scourging at the Pillar: Purity (Mortification of the Senses)

The Crowning with Thorns: Moral Courage (Contempt of the World)

The Carrying of the Cross: Patience

The Crucifixion: Final Perseverance (Self-Sacrifice)

The Glorious Mysteries
The Resurrection: Faith

The Ascension: Hope (Desire for Heaven)

The Descent of the Holy Spirit: Love of God (Wisdom)

The Assumption: Grace of a Happy Death (Devotion to Mary)

The Coronation: Trust in Mary’s Intercession (Eternal Happiness)
