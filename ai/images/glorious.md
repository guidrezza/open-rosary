The composition features intense chiaroscuro lighting, with deep, shadowy backgrounds contrasting against radiant, divine light sources. The color palette is rich and emotive, dominated by deep crimsons, lapis lazuli blues, and golden ochres. The artwork features dynamic movement, swirling drapery, and a classical, painterly texture typical of 17th-century European masterpieces. Subject: The triumphant Christ, radiating brilliant light, rising majestically from an open stone tomb. He holds a banner of victory adorned with a cross. Below him, Roman guards are cowering in terror, shielding their eyes or fallen to the ground, overwhelmed by the divine light and power. The final image should be rendered as a wide landscape composition with a 16:9 aspect ratio.
