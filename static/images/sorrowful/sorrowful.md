Sorrowful Mystery 1: The Agony in the Garden
sorrowful_1_agony
The Agony in the Garden of Gethsemane. High Baroque oil painting, visible brushstrokes, style of Peter Paul Rubens and Caravaggio. VISUAL IDENTITY: 'Eclipse & Tenebrism.' Isolation. LIGHTING: Dual-light conflict. Foreground: Cold, spectral, pale-blue moonlight creating high contrast on Jesus's face. Background: Threatening, warm orange blur of approaching torches. Deep, black shadows. PALETTE: Olive greens, midnight blue, pale skin, and fire orange. SUBJECT: Jesus kneels on jagged grey rock, face up in anguish, sweating drops of blood ('Hematidrosis') staining his tunic. An Angel appears in a ghostly haze offering a Chalice. In foreground shadows, Peter, James, and John are slumped in heavy sleep. Composition isolates Jesus in a pool of cold light. Heavy impasto texture. NO TEXT. NO LABELS. NO PLAQUES. WIDE LANDSCAPE 16:9.

Sorrowful Mystery 3: The Crowning with Thorns
sorrowful_3_crowning
The Crowning with Thorns. High Baroque oil painting, visible brushstrokes, style of Peter Paul Rubens and Caravaggio. VISUAL IDENTITY: 'Eclipse & Tenebrism.' Mockery. LIGHTING: Claustrophobic 'interrogation' spotlight from directly above. Deep shadows in eye sockets. PALETTE: Tyrian Purple (dirty cloak), steel grey, arterial red. SUBJECT: Jesus sits on a stone block, draped in a torn scarlet soldier's cloak, holding a reed. Three soldiers tightly grouped around him, two on the right, one on the left, two soldiers press a woven crown of long, woody thorns onto his head using sticks. The third soldier watching with his hands on his waist. They mockingly laugh. Jesus's face is downcast, showing dignity amidst cruelty. Scene feels crowded and oppressive. Fill the entire 16:9 frame. Sorrowful Mystery 4: The Carrying of the Cross
sorrowful_4_carrying
The Carrying of the Cross (Via Dolorosa). High Baroque oil painting, visible brushstrokes, style of Peter Paul Rubens and Caravaggio. VISUAL IDENTITY: 'Eclipse & Tenebrism.' The Weight. LIGHTING: Oppressive, bleaching midday sun. Not golden/warm, but harsh white heat. Shadows are sharp and hard. PALETTE: Dust browns, faded tunic red, dark textured wood. SUBJECT: Jesus fallen on one knee, crushed under the overwhelming perspective of the Cross beam. He looks up with exhausted eyes. Simon of Cyrene struggles to lift the weight. Virgin Mary reaches out from the side, face sorrowful. Roman soldiers push back a chaotic crowd. The air looks thick with dust. High contrast between the white sun and the dark wood. NO TEXT. Sorrowful Mystery 5: The Crucifixion
sorrowful_5_crucifixion
The Crucifixion of the Lord. High Baroque oil painting, visible brushstrokes, style of Peter Paul Rubens and Caravaggio. VISUAL IDENTITY: 'Eclipse & Tenebrism.' The Death of God. LIGHTING: 'Darkness at Noon.' Sky is apocalyptic storm (bruise-purple/green-black). Single supernatural shaft of light illuminates the pale body of Christ. PALETTE: Black, bruise-purple, corpse-pale skin, red blood. SUBJECT: Jesus hangs on the Cross, head slumped in death. A rough wooden sign (INRI) is above. Blood/water trickles from side. Below, Virgin Mary stands (Stabat Mater) looking up, supported by St. John. Mary Magdalene kneels embracing the bloody wood base. Jerusalem is a silhouette. Absolute solemnity. No floating text, just the painted scene. NO TEXT. NO LABELS. NO PLAQUES. WIDE LANDSCAPE 16:9.
