Joyful Mystery 1: The Annunciation
joyful_1_annunciation
The Annunciation to the Virgin Mary. High Baroque oil painting, visible brushstrokes, style of Peter Paul Rubens, Pietro da Cortona, and Rembrandt. VISUAL IDENTITY: 'Sanctified Earth.' Sacred Domesticity. LIGHTING: 'Rembrandt-esque' directional warmth. A soft, thick beam of golden light enters from a window top left, illuminating dust motes. Shadows are deep, warm brown (burnt umber). PALETTE: Lapis lazuli blue (heavy velvet robe), oak wood, cream, and warm gold. SUBJECT: Archangel Gabriel floats just above the floor, pointing upward, wings soft and feathery. Mary kneels at a heavy wooden prayer desk, hand on chest in surrender. A white Dove descends on the light beam. Texture of the wood floor and heavy fabric is prominent. Visible brushwork, heavy impasto, no digital smoothness. NO TEXT. NO LABELS. NO PLAQUES. WIDE LANDSCAPE 16:9.

joyful_3_nativity
The Nativity of the Lord. High Baroque oil painting, visible brushstrokes, style of Peter Paul Rubens, Pietro da Cortona, and Rembrandt. VISUAL IDENTITY: 'Sanctified Earth.' The Hearth of God. LIGHTING: Lit ENTIRELY by the bioluminescent, warm orange-gold glow radiating from the Christ Child. No external light. Shadows are deep, protective, and warm. Mary kneels, her face glowing from the light of the child. Joseph stands guard. The head of an Ox and Donkey are visible in the warm shadows. The atmosphere is quiet, heavy, and intimate.
