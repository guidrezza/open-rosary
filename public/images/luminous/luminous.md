Luminous Mystery 1: The Baptism in the Jordan
luminous_1_baptism
The Baptism of Christ in the Jordan. High Baroque oil painting, visible brushstrokes, style of Peter Paul Rubens, Pietro da Cortona, and Guido Reni. VISUAL IDENTITY: 'Theophany White.' Revelation. LIGHTING: A dramatic break in the storm clouds creates a vertical column of natural but intense sunlight. It is NOT a laser; it is a shaft of 'divine illumination' painted with heavy white impasto. PALETTE: River moss greens, muddy banks, deep indigo storm clouds, and blinding lead white. SUBJECT: Jesus stands waist-deep in the rushing Jordan River, head bowed, muscular and wet. The water glistens sharply. John the Baptist, wild in camel skin, pours water from a shell. On the bank, two angels wait holding Jesus's red and blue robes. Above, the Holy Spirit (Dove) descends in a halo of white radiance. WIDE LANDSCAPE 16:9. Wet textures, dynamic water flow, sharp contrast. NO TEXT. NO LABELS. NO PLAQUES.

Luminous Mystery 2: The Wedding at Cana
luminous_2_wedding
The Wedding Feast at Cana. High Baroque oil painting, visible brushstrokes, style of Peter Paul Rubens, Pietro da Cortona, and Guido Reni. VISUAL IDENTITY: 'Theophany White.' Glory in the Mundane. LIGHTING: Sharp, crystalline lighting. While the background is ambient, a specific, high-contrast highlight hits the pouring liquid. PALETTE: Rich burgundy, gold brocade, terra-cotta stone, and ruby red. SUBJECT: A lavish banquet. Jesus sits calmly, gesturing toward stone jars. Beside him, Mary whispers to a servant. The focal point is the servant pouring liquid from a jar into a chalice; the liquid transitions from clear water to a glowing, deep ruby-red wine mid-pour. The wine looks like liquid gem, not a neon light. The Master of the Feast looks on in astonishment. WIDE LANDSCAPE 16:9. Opulence, textural contrast between stone and glass. NO TEXT. NO LABELS. Luminous Mystery 3: The Proclamation of the Kingdom
luminous_3_proclamation
The Proclamation of the Kingdom of God. High Baroque oil painting, visible brushstrokes, style of Peter Paul Rubens, Pietro da Cortona, and Guido Reni. VISUAL IDENTITY: 'Theophany White.' The Light of Truth. LIGHTING: High-contrast directional sunlight (Chiaroscuro). It casts sharp, defined shadows under the figures, emphasizing reality. The light is revealing and harsh, not soft. PALETTE: Dusty earth tones, vivid crimson (Jesus's robe), and a dramatic Baroque sky filled with swirling, voluminous clouds opening up to a higher light. SUBJECT: Jesus stands on a rocky outcrop (Mount of Beatitudes), dynamic and commanding. Arms spread, robes blowing. The crowd below is a chaotic mix: Roman soldiers, the sick, and scribes. The light specifically catches the faces of the listeners, highlighting their emotional reactions (tears, shock, skepticism). WIDE LANDSCAPE 16:9. Epic monumental scale, feeling of weight and historical gravity, emotional turbulence. NO TEXT. NO LABELS. NO PLAQUES.

Luminous Mystery 4: The Transfiguration
luminous_4_transfiguration
The Transfiguration on Mount Tabor. High Baroque oil painting, visible brushstrokes, style of Peter Paul Rubens, Pietro da Cortona, and Guido Reni. VISUAL IDENTITY: 'Theophany White.' Uncreated Light. LIGHTING: The light source IS the face and body of Jesus. It is very bright, thick 'titanium white' brilliance that casts the rest of the mountain into deep, heavy shadow. PALETTE: Blinding white, cool blue, and deep, dark rock tones (almost black). SUBJECT: Jesus floats in the air, his garments painting in dazzling white strokes. He converses with Moses (Tablets) and Elijah (Scroll). Below them, Peter, James, and John are sprawled violently on the ground, shielding their eyes. The contrast is extreme—the top of the painting is exploded with light, the bottom is heavy with shadow. WIDE LANDSCAPE 16:9. Supernatural power, blinding brightness, thick paint texture. NO TEXT. NO LABELS. NO PLAQUES.

Luminous Mystery 5: The Institution of the Eucharist
luminous_5_institution
The Institution of the Holy Eucharist. High Baroque oil painting, visible brushstrokes, style of Peter Paul Rubens, Pietro da Cortona, and Guido Reni. VISUAL IDENTITY: 'Theophany White.' The Hidden God. LIGHTING: Tenebrism. The room is dark, lit ONLY by the Host in Christ's hands. The bread does not 'glow' like a lightbulb, but emits a soft, mystical, pearl-white luminescence that illuminates the faces of the Apostles nearby. PALETTE: Warm wood browns, deep reds, and the pale, spectral white of the Host. SUBJECT: The Last Supper. Jesus sits center, elevating the broken bread. The Apostles lean in, their faces carved out of the darkness by the white light of the Eucharist. In the far foreground shadow, Judas turns away, stepping into the black void. The contrast between the White Bread and the Black Shadows. WIDE LANDSCAPE 16:9. Intimacy, sacred mystery, shadow play. NO TEXT. NO LABELS. NO PLAQUES.
