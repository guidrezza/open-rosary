Glorious Mystery 1: The Resurrection
glorious_1_resurrection
The Resurrection of Christ. High Baroque oil painting, visible brushstrokes, style of Peter Paul Rubens, Pietro da Cortona, and Tiepolo. VISUAL IDENTITY: 'Empyrean Radiance.' The triumph of light. LIGHTING: 'Aurora' lighting—cool misty blue shadows vs. blinding warm white/gold highlights. NO harsh black shadows. PALETTE: Pearl white, iridescent opal, solar gold, and pale dawn blue. SUBJECT: The glorified Christ levitates above an open stone sarcophagus. Christ's face is dynamic, gazing upward toward heaven with ecstatic energy, rather than stoic. He is draped in a white shroud that flows like liquid light. He holds the 'Vexillum' (white banner with red cross). His five wounds emit soft, hazy radiance (NOT solid beams). Below, Roman Centurions in armor recoil, shielding eyes. The painting must have extreme visible brushstrokes and heavy, tactile impasto texture throughout, avoiding any digital smoothness. NO TEXT. NO LABELS. NO PLAQUES. WIDE LANDSCAPE 16:9.

Glorious Mystery 2: The Ascension
glorious_2_ascension
The Ascension of the Lord. High Baroque oil painting, visible brushstrokes, style of Peter Paul Rubens, Pietro da Cortona, and Tiepolo. VISUAL IDENTITY: 'Empyrean Radiance.' Vertical ascent. LIGHTING: A soft, diffused golden 'Glory' (Shekhinah) fills the top 80% of the canvas. The bottom is earthy, but fading. PALETTE: Cerulean blue (sky), cloud white, gold leaf, and faint terracotta. SUBJECT: Christ is high in the center, foreshortened, entering a swirling vortex of golden clouds. He looks down, blessing the earth. Below, on the Mount of Olives, the 11 Apostles and the Virgin Mary (in bright lapis blue) form a circle, gesturing upward. The focus is on the upward draft and the dissolving of the physical body into glory. Visible canvas grain and oil texture. NO TEXT. NO LABELS. NO PLAQUES. WIDE LANDSCAPE 16:9.

Glorious Mystery 3: The Descent of the Holy Spirit (Pentecost)
glorious_3_descent
The Descent of the Holy Spirit at Pentecost. High Baroque oil painting, visible brushstrokes, style of Peter Paul Rubens, Pietro da Cortona, and Tiepolo. VISUAL IDENTITY: 'Empyrean Radiance.' The Light dissolves the Room. LIGHTING: The architecture is blown out by supernatural light. The light source is the Spirit, casting a warm, vibrant orange-gold glow. PALETTE: 'Pentecostal Red' (vermilion), burnt orange, gold, and white. SUBJECT: The Virgin Mary sits centrally as 'Sedes Sapientiae'. The Apostles surround her in ecstatic joy. Above, the Dove of the Holy Spirit emits distinct 'cloven tongues' of fire hovering over each head. The drapery swirls violently. The ceiling of the room seems to dissolve into heavenly light. Heavy impasto texture. NO TEXT. NO LABELS. NO PLAQUES. WIDE LANDSCAPE 16:9.

Glorious Mystery 4: The Assumption of Mary
glorious_4_assumption
The Assumption of the Virgin Mary. High Baroque oil painting, visible brushstrokes, style of Peter Paul Rubens, Pietro da Cortona, and Tiepolo. VISUAL IDENTITY: 'Empyrean Radiance.' Weightless Ascent. LIGHTING: Crystalline, noon-day brightness. The air feels thin and pure. PALETTE: Lapis lazuli (Mary), rose pink (Angels), and brilliant cloud white. SUBJECT: The Virgin Mary is being carried upward on a dense, fluffy cloud bank by a swarm of muscular, joyful Cherubim. Her arms are outstretched. At the very bottom, a stone sarcophagus overflows with white lilies and red roses. The composition is a spiraling 'sotto in su' (seen from below). The figures float in a sea of blue sky. Visible brushwork. NO TEXT. NO LABELS. NO PLAQUES. WIDE LANDSCAPE 16:9.

Glorious Mystery 5: The Coronation of Mary
glorious_5_coronation
The Coronation of Mary as Queen of Heaven. High Baroque oil painting, visible brushstrokes, style of Peter Paul Rubens, Pietro da Cortona, and Tiepolo. VISUAL IDENTITY: 'Empyrean Radiance.' The Beatific Vision. LIGHTING: Absolute saturation of light. NO shadows. A 'Glory' (halo) surrounds the group. PALETTE: Imperial Gold, Royal Purple, and Celestial Blue. SUBJECT: A scene in Empyrean Heaven (no earth). Mary, clothed in sun-woven garments, kneels on a cloud throne. God the Father (left) and Jesus Christ (right) place a crown of twelve stars on her head. The Holy Spirit (Dove) hovers above. The background is a dense host of angels dissolving into golden light. Asymmetrical, dynamic composition (not stiffly centered). NO TEXT. NO LABELS. NO PLAQUES. WIDE LANDSCAPE 16:9.
