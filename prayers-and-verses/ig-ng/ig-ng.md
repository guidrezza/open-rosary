# Ntuziaka Rosary (Igbo - Nigeria)

**Ntụgharị:** CBCN (Catholic Bishops' Conference of Nigeria)
**Akwụkwọ Nsọ:** Bible Nso (Catholic Edition)

## I. Usoro Ihe Omimi (Mystery Schedule)

_Rịba ama: Usoro a bụ nke a na-emekarị na Nzukọ Nsọ Katolik. Agbanyeghị, Rosary bụ ekpere onwe onye, ị nwere ike ikpe ihe omimi ọbụla n'ụbọchị ọbụla dịka mmasị gị siri dị._

- **Mọnde:** Ihe Omimi nke Ọṅụ (Joyful)
- **Tuzdee:** Ihe Omimi nke Ihe Mwute (Sorrowful)
- **Wenezde:** Ihe Omimi nke Ebube (Glorious)
- **Tọzdee:** Ihe Omimi nke Ihe (Luminous)
- **Fraịdee:** Ihe Omimi nke Ihe Mwute (Sorrowful)
- **Satọdee:** Ihe Omimi nke Ọṅụ (Joyful)
- **Sande:** Ihe Omimi nke Ebube (Glorious)

## IV. Ekpere na Akwụkwọ Nsọ

**S. Ime Akara Obe**
N'aha Nna, na Nwa, na Mmụọ Nsọ. Amen.

**C. Kredo (Apostles' Creed)**
Ekwere m na Chineke, Nna onye pụrụ ime ihe niile, Onye kere eluigwe na ụwa. M na-ekwere na Jesu Kristi, Sọ otu Nwa ya, Onyenwe anyị, Onye a tụụrụ ime ya site na Mmụọ Nsọ, amụọ ya na Virgin Maria, O tara ahụhụ n'okpuru Pọntius Paịlet, a kpọgburu ya n'obe, O nwụrụ ma lie ya; O ridaa n'ala ndị nwụrụ anwụ; n'ụbọchị nke atọ O siri na ndị nwụrụ anwụ bilie; O rịgoro n'eluigwe, ma nọrọ n'aka nri nke Chineke Nna onye pụrụ ime ihe niile; site n'ebe ahụ Ọ ga-abịa ikpe ndị dị ndụ na ndị nwụrụ anwụ ikpe.
Ekwere m na Mmụọ Nsọ, na Nzukọ Nsọ Katolik, na mmekọrịta nke ndị nsọ, na mgbaghara nke mmehie, na mbilite n'ọnwụ nke anụ ahụ, na ndụ ebighi ebi. Amen.

**P. Nna Anyị (Our Father)**
Nna anyị nke bi n'eluigwe, ka aha Gị dị nsọ; ka alaeze Gị bịa; ka eme uche Gị n'ụwa dịka e si eme ya n'eluigwe. Nye anyị taa nri nke ụbọchị anyị; gbaghara anyị mmehie anyị, dịka anyị si agbaghara ndị mehiere anyị; ekwela ka anyị banye na ọnwụnwa, kama zọpụta anyị n'ajọ ihe. Amen.

**A. Ekele Maria (Hail Mary)**
Ekele Maria, i jupụtara na grasịa, Onyenwe anyị nọnyeere gị. Ị gọziri agọzi n'ime ụmụ nwanyị niile, ma a gọziri nwa nke afọ gị, Jesu. Maria Dị Nsọ, Nne nke Chineke, kpeere anyị ndị mmehie ekpere, ugbu a na n'oge ọnwụ anyị. Amen.

**G. Otito Dịrị Nna (Glory Be)**
Otito dịrị Nna, na Nwa, na Mmụọ Nsọ. Dịka ọ dị na mmalite, otu ahụ ka ọ dị ugbu a, otu ahụ ka ọ ga-adịgide, ụwa ebighi ebi. Amen.

**J. Ekpere Fatima**
Jesu m, gbaghara anyị mmehie anyị, zọpụta anyị n'ọkụ nke hell, duga mkpụrụ obi niile n'eluigwe, karịsịa ndị nwere mkpa ebere Gị.

**R. Ekele Eze Nwanyi (Hail Holy Queen)**
Ekele Eze Nwanyi, Nne nke ebere, ndụ anyị, ụtọ anyị na olileanya anyị. Anyị na-akpọku gị, anyị ụmụ Iv a chụrụ n'ala ọzọ; anyị na-ezitere gị ude na akwa anyị n'ime ndagwurugwu anya mmiri a. Ya mere, onye na-arịọchitere anyị, tụgharịa anya ebere gị n'ebe anyị nọ; ma mgbe nchụpụ a gasịrị, gosi anyị Jesu, nwa a gọziri agọzi nke afọ gị. O onye ebere, O onye ịhụnanya, O Maria Virgin dị ụtọ.

V. Kpeere anyị ekpere, O Nne Nsọ nke Chineke.
R. Ka anyị wee ruo eru inweta nkwa nke Kristi.

**O. Ekpere Mmechi**
Ka anyị kpee ekpere. O Chineke, onye otu Ọmụmụ Nwa Gị, site na ndụ Ya, ọnwụ Ya na mbilite n'ọnwụ Ya, zụtara anyị ụgwọ ọrụ nke ndụ ebighi ebi; anyị na-arịọ Gị, nye anyị amara ka anyị, site n'ịtụgharị uche n'ihe omimi ndị a nke Rosary Nsọ nke Virgin Maria Dị Nsọ, anyị nwere ike iṅomi ihe ha nwere ma nweta ihe ha kwere na nkwa. Site na Kristi Onyenwe anyị. Amen.

**M. Ihe Omimi (Mysteries)**

**Ihe Omimi nke Ọṅụ (Joyful)**

1. **Nnwaata nke Onyenwe Anyị:** "Mmụọ Ozi ahụ sịrị ya, 'Atụla egwu, Maria, n'ihi na i nwetala amara n'ihu Chineke. Lee, ị ga-atụrụ ime, mụọ nwa nwoke, ị ga-akpọkwa aha ya Jesu.'" (Luk 1:30-31)
   - **Mkpụrụ:** Ịdị Umeala n'Obi
2. **Nleta nke Maria:** "Elisabet jupụtara na Mmụọ Nsọ wee jiri oké olu tie mkpu sị, 'Ị gọziri agọzi n'ime ụmụ nwanyị, a gọzikwara nwa nke afọ gị!'" (Luk 1:41-42)
   - **Mkpụrụ:** Ịhụ Mmadụ n'Anya
3. **Ọmụmụ nke Jesu:** "Ọ mụrụ nwa nwoke ya nke mbụ, kechie ya n'akwa, ma tinye ya n'ihe ebe anụ na-eri nri, n'ihi na ọ dịghị ohere dịịrị ha n'ụlọ ndị ọbịa." (Luk 2:7)
   - **Mkpụrụ:** Ịbụ Ogbenye na Mmụọ
4. **Ngosi nke Jesu n'Ụlọ Nsọ:** "Mgbe ụbọchị ha ga-eme emume ịdị ọcha dịka iwu Mozis siri dị ruru, ha kpọọrọ ya gaa Jerusalem ka ha gosi ya Onyenwe anyị." (Luk 2:22)
   - **Mkpụrụ:** Ige Ntị na Iwu Chineke
5. **Nchọta nke Jesu n'Ụlọ Nsọ:** "Mgbe ụbọchị atọ gasịrị, ha hụrụ ya n'ụlọ nsọ, ka ọ nọ n'etiti ndị nkuzi, na-ege ha ntị ma na-ajụ ha ajụjụ." (Luk 2:46)
   - **Mkpụrụ:** Ọṅụ na Jesu

**Ihe Omimi nke Ihe (Luminous)**

1. **Baptism nke Jesu na Jọdan:** "Otu olu sitere n'eluigwe sị, 'Nke a bụ Ọkpara m m hụrụ n'anya, onye ihe ya na-atọ m ụtọ.'" (Matiu 3:17)
   - **Mkpụrụ:** Imeghe Obi nye Mmụọ Nsọ
2. **Agbamakwụkwọ na Kana:** "Nne ya gwara ndị na-ejere ya ozi, 'Ihe ọ bụla ọ gwara unu, mee nụ ya.'" (Jọn 2:5)
   - **Mkpụrụ:** Ijekwuru Jesu site na Maria
3. **Nkwupụta nke Alaeze Chineke:** "Oge eruola, Alaeze Chineke dị nso; chegharịanụ, ma kwere n'Ozi Ọma." (Mak 1:15)
   - **Mkpụrụ:** Nchegharị na Okwukwe
4. **Mgbanwe nke Jesu:** "Ọ gbanwere n'ihu ha; ihu ya na-enwu ka anyanwụ, uwe ya wee dị ọcha ka ìhè." (Matiu 17:2)
   - **Mkpụrụ:** Ọchịchọ nke Ịdị Nsọ
5. **Ntọala nke Eucharist:** "Jesu weere achịcha, gọzie ya, nyawasịa ya; o wee nye ndị na-eso ụzọ ya sị, 'Naranụ, rie; nke a bụ ahụ m.'" (Matiu 26:26)
   - **Mkpụrụ:** Ofufe nke Eucharist

**Ihe Omimi nke Ihe Mwute (Sorrowful)**

1. **Ahụhụ n'Ubi Gethsemane:** "O nwere oké mwute n'obi, o kpesie ekpere ike karị; ọsụsọ ya wee dịka nnukwu mkpụrụ ọbara na-adada n'ala." (Luk 22:44)
   - **Mkpụrụ:** Nchegharị maka Mmehie
2. **Ịpia Jesu Ihe:** "Mgbe ahụ Paịlet kpọọrọ Jesu ma pịa ya ihe." (Jọn 19:1)
   - **Mkpụrụ:** Imerie Anụ Ahụ
3. **Ikpube Jesu Okpu Ogwu:** "Ndị agha kpara okpu ogwu kpubere ya n'isi, yikwasịkwa ya uwe odo odo." (Jọn 19:2)
   - **Mkpụrụ:** Obi Ike
4. **Ibu Obe:** "Jesu buuru obe ya pụọ, gaa n'ebe a na-akpọ Ebe Okpokoro Isi." (Jọn 19:17)
   - **Mkpụrụ:** Ndidi
5. **Ọnwụ Jesu n'Obe:** "Jesu ji oké olu tie mkpu sị, 'Nna, n'aka Gị ka m na-enyefe mmụọ m.' Mgbe o kwusịrị nke a, o wee kubie ume." (Luk 23:46)
   - **Mkpụrụ:** Ịhụ Chineke n'Anya na Nzọpụta nke Mkpụrụ Obi

**Ihe Omimi nke Ebube (Glorious)**

1. **Mbilite n'Ọnwụ:** "Ọ nọghị ebe a; n'ihi na o bilitewo dịka o kwuru." (Matiu 28:6)
   - **Mkpụrụ:** Okwukwe
2. **Nrịgo Elu:** "Mgbe Onyenwe anyị Jesu gwara ha okwu, a kpọọrọ ya gaa n'eluigwe, o wee nọdụ n'aka nri nke Chineke." (Mak 16:19)
   - **Mkpụrụ:** Olileanya
3. **Mgbadate nke Mmụọ Nsọ:** "Ha niile wee jupụta na Mmụọ Nsọ, malite ikwu okwu n'asụsụ ndị ọzọ." (Ọrụ Ndị Ozi 2:4)
   - **Mkpụrụ:** Ịhụ Chineke n'Anya
4. **Mbuli Elu nke Maria:** "N'ihi na Onye Pụrụ Ime Ihe Niile emerewo m nnukwu ihe; aha ya dịkwa nsọ." (Luk 1:49)
   - **Mkpụrụ:** Amara nke Ọnwụ Ọma
5. **Okpu Eze nke Maria:** "A hụrụ nnukwu ihe ịrịba ama n'eluigwe: otu nwanyị nke yi anyanwụ dịka uwe, ọnwa nọkwa n'okpuru ụkwụ ya, na okpu nke kpakpando iri na abụọ n'isi ya." (Mkpughe 12:1)
   - **Mkpụrụ:** Ntụkwasị Obi na Maria
