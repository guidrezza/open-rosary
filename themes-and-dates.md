# Themes, Liturgical Time, and Holy Days

## Theme Aesthetics
- **Base**: Dark Gray / Neutral 900.
- **Effect**: Warping lights/gradients that wrap around the base, inheriting the theme color.
- **Palette**: See below.

## Themes & Colors
### Primary Seasons
- **Advent / Lent**: Violet (✝️)
- **Christmas / Easter**: White (🕊️)
- **Ordinary Time**: Green (🌿)

### Special Feasts & Solemnities
- **High Solemnities**: Gold (✨)
- **Paschal Triduum**: White and Red (🌹)
- **Pentecost / Martyr**: Red (🔥)
- **Gaudete / Laetare**: Rose (🌸)
- **Votive / Solemn**: Silver (🩶)

### 2. Flexible Dates & Seasons (Calculated)
#### Advent Cycle
- **Season of Advent**: 4 Sundays before Christmas. (Violet ✝️)
- **Gaudete Sunday**: 3rd Sunday of Advent. (Rose 🌸)

#### Christmas Cycle
- **Christmas Season**: Dec 25 - Baptism of the Lord. (White 🕊️)
- **Holy Family**: Sunday within Octave (or Dec 30 if no Sunday). (White 🕊️)
- **Epiphany**: Jan 6 (or nearest Sunday depending on region, sticking to Jan 6 for fixed logic).
- **Baptism of the Lord**: Sunday after Epiphany. (White 🕊️)

#### Lent Cycle
- **Ash Wednesday**: 46 days before Easter. (Violet ✝️)
- **Season of Lent**: Ash Wed - Holy Thursday. (Violet ✝️)
- **Laetare Sunday**: 4th Sunday of Lent. (Rose 🌸)
- **Palm Sunday**: Sunday before Easter. (Red 🔥)

#### Paschal Triduum
- **Holy Thursday**: Thursday before Easter. (White/Red 🌹)
- **Good Friday**: Friday before Easter. (White/Red 🌹)
- **Holy Saturday**: Saturday before Easter. (White/Red 🌹)
- **Easter Vigil**: Night before Easter. (Gold ✨)

#### Easter Cycle
- **Easter Sunday**: 1st Sunday after full moon > Spring Equinox. (Gold ✨)
- **Easter Octave (Divine Mercy)**: 2nd Sunday of Easter. (White 🕊️)
- **Easter Season**: Easter - Pentecost. (White 🕊️)
- **Ascension**: 40 days after Easter (Thursday). (Gold ✨)
- **Pentecost**: 50 days after Easter. (Red 🔥)

#### Ordinary Time
- **Period 1**: Mon after Baptism of Lord -> Ash Wed. (Green 🌿)
- **Period 2**: Mon after Pentecost -> Advent. (Green 🌿)
- **Trinity Sunday**: Sunday after Pentecost. (Gold ✨)
- **Corpus Christi**: Thursday after Trinity. (Gold ✨)
- **Sacred Heart**: Friday after Corpus Christi week (19 days after Pentecost). (White 🕊️)
- **Christ the King**: Last Sunday of Ordinary Time. (Gold ✨)

### Logic Strategy
1.  **Calculate Key Anchors**: Easter (Year), Advent Start (Year).
2.  **Derive Flex Dates**: Ash Wed, Pentecost, etc.
3.  **Check Priority**:
    1.  Fixed High Feasts (e.g. Christmas Day, Immaculate Conception).
    2.  Calculated Solemnities (e.g. Easter, Pentecost, Trinity).
    3.  Specific Sundays (Gaudete, Laetare).
    4.  Seasons (Lent, Easter, Advent).
    5.  Ordinary Time.
